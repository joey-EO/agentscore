Scoring logic

Production: 0–2=1, 3–5=3, 6–11=5, 12–23=7, 24–35=9, 36+=10.

Accessibility: Starts at 10 after an outreach attempt; subtracts 0.75 per additional attempt, 0.5 per proactive reschedule, and 2 per cancellation/no-show; minimum 1. This is a provisional model.

Momentum: Select a documented milestone (0, 3, 5, 7, 9, or 10). This is a provisional model.

Overall: Mean of available production, accessibility, and momentum scores. If accessibility is unknown, the score is provisional and based on production and momentum. A 7+ score is qualified, subject to review.

Investment alert: Three or more hosted coffees, lunches, or events with no real opportunity (momentum below 7) triggers a caution. Investment is not added to the qualification score.
